# Pixelion Captions Wiki

Pixelion Captions adds live transcription and translation to OBS Studio. It reads the audio from your OBS program mix and shows captions in a source you can place and style in your scene.

Pixelion Captions is a third-party plugin developed by [Pixel Solutions](https://pixelsolutions.biz/). It is not part of OBS Studio and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the OBS Project.

## Installation

Pick your operating system:

- **[Installing on Windows](Installing-on-Windows)** — installer (`.exe`) or manual ZIP
- **[Installing on macOS](Installing-on-macOS)** — signed, notarized `.plugin` bundle

## What you need

| | Requirement |
|---|---|
| OBS Studio | Tested with 32.0.1 (built against 31.1.1 dependencies). Other versions are not confirmed. |
| Platform | Windows x64, or macOS 13.7 (Ventura) and later (universal: Apple silicon + Intel) |
| Account | A free Pixelion.ai account |
| Network | An internet connection for live transcription and translation |
| Translation | A Pixelion Pro plan (transcription works on the free plan) |

Available minutes and session limits depend on your subscription.

## After installing

Once the plugin is installed and OBS lists it, see the [README](https://github.com/pixelsolutions/pixelion-captions/blob/main/README.md#start-using-captions) for how to sign in, add a caption source, and start a session.
